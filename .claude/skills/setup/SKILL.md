description: First-run interview that records what the person wants from their job search
disable-model-invocation: true
---
Interview the person about what they want next, then write `vault/preferences.md`. If that file already exists, read it first and ask only what has changed.

Ask one question at a time, in plain language, and follow up on vague answers. Cover: the kinds of role they want and why, seniority, where they can work and whether remote matters, minimum pay and currency, kinds of company they want and want to avoid, dealbreakers, how soon they need to start, languages they work in, and anything about their last job they never want to repeat.

Keep it to about five minutes. If they skip something, leave it out instead of guessing.

Write the file in the person's own words under these headings: `# What I'm looking for`, `## Roles`, `## Location and remote`, `## Pay`, `## Companies`, `## Dealbreakers`, `## Timing`. Show it to them and ask what's wrong before finishing.

Only write inside `vault/`.
